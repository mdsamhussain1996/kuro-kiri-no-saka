# KURO: Kiri-no-Saka — Boss Design & Moveset (`docs/BOSSES.md`)

## 1. Kurogane, the Gatekeeper (Chapter IV · 56 HP)
- **Role:** Tests Parry, Dash-through, and Punish Windows.
- **Defense:** Armoured during neutral movement (`0` dmg + clink). Vulnerable (`×2` dmg) during **Slam Recovery** (`1.15s`), **Shoulder Charge Recovery**, or **Perfect Parry Stagger** (`2.0s`).
- **Moveset:**
  - *Floor Ink Sweep:* `0.5s` wind-up + vermilion glint → `14 u/s` floor wave (jump over; parryable in Phase 3).
  - *Heavy Slam:* `0.55s` overhead wind-up → `2 HP` slam → `1.15s` embedded blade punish window.
  - *Shoulder Charge:* `0.5s` crouch telegraph → `16 u/s` rush → exposed back on wall/stop.
  - *Phase 2 (<60% HP):* Roar + Torii Crossbar Leap & triple shockwave.
  - *Phase 3 (<25% HP):* Enraged flurry; sweeps become parryable for a 2.0s Ippitsu window.
- **Phase Checkpoint:** Respawning in Phase 2/3 restarts at that phase's HP threshold for the first 2 retries.

## 2. Lady Shirayuki, the Mist Weaver (Chapter VI · 44 HP)
- **Role:** Tests Spatial Reading, Deflection, and Bell Mist-Clearing.
- **Defense:** Armoured while shrouded; attacking outside her `1.35s` post-volley punish window triggers a clink and mist teleport.
- **Silhouette:** Inverted ink (white-robed maiden with dark outline & vermilion hairpin) with orbiting ink-mirror decoys.
- **Moveset:**
  - *Spirit Needle Fan:* `0.5s` telegraph → 3–5 deflectable spirit needles (`13.5 u/s`) → `1.35s` punish window.
  - *Phase 2 (<50% HP):* Summons mist & faster `2.2s` volley cadence; ring **Bell (`F`)** to clear mist and expose the real reflection.

## 3. Lord Sumi-Ou, Warlord of the Iron Brush (Chapter VIII · 64 HP)
- **Role:** Final Exam — combines dodging, jumping, parrying, and Ippitsu.
- **Defense:** Armoured except during `1.3s` post-sweep recovery or weak-point Ippitsu strikes.
- **Moveset:**
  - *Erupting Ink Pillar:* `1.0s` vermilion floor seal telegraph → erupting ink column.
  - *Floor Shockwave Sweep:* `13.5 u/s` wave + dual-brush strike → `1.3s` punish window.
  - *Phase 2 (<50% HP):* "Living Calligraphy" — triple ink pillars + rapid sweeps.
- **Reward:** Defeating Lord Sumi-Ou restores **Scroll 3 of 3**, triggers the Sunrise Parchment Wash, and unlocks **EndCard** + post-campaign modes.
